# Liquidity and Derivatives

Watch round numbers, prior highs/lows, obvious support/resistance and clustered stops.

Preferred long: support sweep -> reclaim -> 15m/1h confirmation.
Preferred short: resistance sweep -> failed expansion -> loss of level -> 15m/1h confirmation.

Interpret jointly:
- price up + OI up + moderate funding + volume up: healthier expansion
- price up + OI down + large short liquidations: squeeze risk
- price up + OI surges + extreme positive funding: crowded-long risk
- price down + OI down + large long liquidations: deleveraging; avoid chasing into support

Also inspect basis, 25-delta skew, ATM IV, term structure and major options expiry/gamma zones when available.
