# Japan Carry-Risk Module

Monitor:
- BOJ policy/guidance
- JGB 2Y, 10Y, 30Y, 40Y
- 2s10s and 10s30s curves
- UST2Y-JGB2Y and UST10Y-JGB10Y spreads
- 1h/4h/24h/5d changes in spreads
- USD/JPY and JPY implied volatility
- Nikkei 225 / TOPIX
- MOF rate-check/intervention news

JGB yield rise alone is NOT automatically bearish for BTC.

Escalate carry-unwind risk when JGB yields rise, US-Japan spreads compress rapidly, JPY appreciates sharply (USD/JPY falls), Japanese/global equities weaken, and crypto OI/price weaken together.

If BOJ/JGB headlines are hawkish but USD/JPY remains firm and BTC refuses to fall, treat the price resilience as important contrary evidence.
